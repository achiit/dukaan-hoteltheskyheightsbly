About us

Two partners, one new address.

GA
AG

Gourav Arora & Aman Gupta

We opened The Sky Heights in 2025 because Lohiya Vihar and CB Ganj Road needed one place where a family could eat properly, stay the night, and book a hall without running around town.

Our kitchen is pure veg, and we are proud of it. Mama-Bhanja Paneer is the dish people ask for first, then the Kurkure Momos. We keep tasting every batch ourselves, because we are new and we want your second visit.

If something is not right, tell us at the table or WhatsApp us. We would rather fix it the same day.

  • AC double-bed rooms with marble flooring
  • Separate party hall for functions
